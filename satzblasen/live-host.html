Everyone plays on their own phone, on the same grid at the same time, and connects neighbouring bubbles into sentences. The player with the most words connected wins the round; ties go to fewer wrong attempts, then to the earlier finish. Students join with a nickname, not their real name. When you end the game, the room and everything in it is deleted.
