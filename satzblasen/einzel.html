Sentence Bubbles

Connect neighbouring bubbles, in order, to build German sentences. Use every word. Be careful: a sentence that is correct on its own can leave other words with nobody to join.

Tap bubbles one after another. Press “Check sentence” to confirm. Tap the last bubble to take it back, or an earlier bubble to step back to it.
