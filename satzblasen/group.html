Sentence Bubbles: group mode

Teams

The teams take turns. The teacher taps the bubbles of the team’s sentence, in order (diagonal neighbours count), and presses “Check sentence”. Tapping the last bubble takes it back.

30 s

The words a sentence can start with are coloured. A Tip button shows the words that can come next; a word that was tipped scores no point.

A correct sentence scores one point per word. A wrong attempt is a strike and ends the turn; a team with three strikes is out for this round. A sentence that is not in the list can be accepted by the teacher anyway.
