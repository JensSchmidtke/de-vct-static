Letter Hunt: teacher

New room

2:00

Students join with a nickname, not their real name. Only words of the chapter vocabulary, with their forms, count. Several of them are hidden in straight lines, as in a word search; others need bent paths. Points depend on the length of the word. When you end the game, the room and everything in it is deleted.
